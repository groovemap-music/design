# GrooveMap provider licensing and commercial use

Reviewed 24 October 2026. Covers GrooveMap’s two current sources and twelve candidate providers.

Commercial use is broadly permitted for CC0 data, but a provider can offer different licenses for catalog metadata, community contributions, images, and hosted APIs. The Commercial column states the conditions for each provider; permission to use data in an app does not automatically permit bulk ingestion, resale, or redistribution through GrooveMap’s API.

## Commercial use conditions

**Commercial permission and bulk ingestion are different rights.** A license can allow a paid app while limiting retention, sublicensing, data resale, independent catalog creation, or access through a third-party API. Agreements for Last.fm, setlist.fm, SecondHandSongs and other restricted services should explicitly cover the intended ingestion, derived outputs, display and redistribution.

**Share-alike applies to covered material and adaptations, not automatically to every part of GrooveMap.** Keep source provenance and license boundaries so CC0 metadata, share-alike data, restricted fields and copyrighted images remain identifiable. [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

**MusicBrainz tags need field-level handling.** Genre names belong to the core vocabulary, while community genre associations and tags are supplementary. MetaBrainz’s dataset overview labels its JSON exports CC0, while its database overview lists tags as supplementary; confirm the applicable export contents and rights before treating nonempty tag fields as commercially reusable. [Database categories](https://musicbrainz.org/doc/MusicBrainz_Database), [export overview](https://metabrainz.org/datasets/postgres-dumps)

**Apple’s feed route has changed.** The current Enterprise Partner Feed page says Apple Music and iTunes Music data are no longer available through EPF and directs app developers to Apple Music Feed. Use that current notice rather than the older EPF description farther down the same page. [Current feed notice](https://performance-partners.apple.com/epf)

## License definitions

| Term | Commercial meaning |
| --- | --- |
| CC0 | Public-domain dedication. Commercial copying, modification and redistribution are permitted without attribution or share-alike obligations under CC0. Trademark, privacy and other third-party rights remain separate. [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| CC BY-SA 3.0 | Commercial use is permitted. Give appropriate attribution and follow the license’s share-alike conditions when distributing adaptations. [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) |
| CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 | Commercial use is not permitted under this license. Noncommercial reuse requires attribution and share-alike for distributed adaptations; a separate commercial license can provide different rights. [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/) |
| Provider agreement | Rights depend on the accepted contract, plan, permitted purpose and any written exceptions. A working API key or paid subscription alone does not establish unrestricted commercial reuse. |

The commercial classifications summarize the cited public terms as of the review date. Apply the terms for the actual data, acquisition channel and intended use; separately negotiated agreements can change the result.
