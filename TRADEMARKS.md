You may:

- truthfully refer to GrooveMap when discussing, reviewing, teaching about, reporting on, or linking to the project;
- accurately describe something as compatible with, for, or derived from GrooveMap when the description is not confusing and carries an equally prominent statement that it is not an official GrooveMap offering and is not sponsored or endorsed by GrooveMap;
- use the name for a community event, user group, or other noncommercial community material when the use does not suggest official status, sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement; and
- reproduce an unmodified GrooveMap logo when reasonably necessary for truthful identification, provided the logo is subordinate to your own name and identity.

Separate written permission is required to:

- use a GrooveMap name, logo, or domain as the name or source identifier of another product, service, organization, or commercial event;
- adopt a confusingly similar name, logo, domain, social account, or other identifier;
- claim or imply official status, sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement; or
- use a modified GrooveMap logo as another project's primary identity, or use a GrooveMap identifier primarily for merchandising or fundraising.

When permission is required, contact the maintainers through the GrooveMap GitHub organization before using the identifier. Permission from one contributor does not convey rights held by another person or entity.
